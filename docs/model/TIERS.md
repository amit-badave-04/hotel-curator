## 1-3. Subset invariance, monotonicity, truth tables

Checked by tests, not by this report: `tests/unit/test_demo_subset.py` recomputes the tiers of the committed demo subset from its stored review signals and requires them to equal the served tiers; `tests/unit/test_tiers.py` holds the truth tables and the property tests (with the review count and month spread held fixed, one more complaint never raises a ranked tier, and one more praise never lowers one except in two cases, both at the 10 opinions needed for a grade: it lifts a non-essential attribute to 10 opinions and the new grade is a material Weak, a new catch; or it lifts an essential that is Weak on expected counts to 10 opinions on hard labels, where it is Weak too, so Fair falls to Caution by rule 2; ADR 006, errata E2 and E6); `tests/unit/test_grades.py` holds the grade truth tables.
