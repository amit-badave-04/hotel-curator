# External check: the annotator panel versus people

_Generated by `curator label external-score`. Only aggregates are committed; the source reviews have an unverified licence and stay in a gitignored directory._

The v2 labels come from an LLM annotator panel (ADR 004). To see how the panel compares with people, the same panel and guideline labeled the 442 unique reviews of *TripAdvisor Annotated* (Marcheggiani and Titov), whose sentence-level aspect labels were made by human annotators. 73 of those reviews were labeled by three people each, which allows a like-for-like comparison: does the panel agree with each person about as well as the people agree with each other?

Caveats: different site and decade, whole reviews instead of Liked/Disliked parts, and a different guideline (the source has no noise or wifi aspect, so 12 of the 16 labels are checked). Human labels include implied opinions (`ip`/`in` codes); the explicit-only variant is in `data/labels/external_check.json`.

## Reading

- Recall against people ranges from 0.81 to 0.98. Precision is 0.82 to 0.92 on praise and 0.57 to 0.77 on complaints: the panel marks mild or passing complaints the original annotators left out. Complaint rates estimated from v2 labels therefore lean high in absolute terms; comparisons between hotels are affected less, to the extent the over-marking is similar across hotels (not measured).
- These numbers are about the *labels*. Model quality is measured against the panel's gold sets in `docs/model/COMPARISON.md`.
- A direct human audit of 100 v2 gold reviews is prepared for the owner (`docs/labeling/HUMAN_AUDIT.md`, docs/BLOCKERS.md B3).
