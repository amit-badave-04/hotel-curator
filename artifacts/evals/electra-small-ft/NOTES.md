Trained with batches drawn at random. Length-bucketed batching (batches of similar-length parts, in shuffled order; `ml/encoder.py::length_bucketed_batches`) was added after this run and used for every later encoder. Bucketing changes the batch composition and the CPU time, not the hyperparameters, which are fixed in advance (plan section 3.6). The run was not repeated, so its training time is not directly comparable with the other encoders.
